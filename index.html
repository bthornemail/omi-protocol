How It Works

The Omi-Ring has 5040 slots. Every relation belongs to exactly one slot — determined by the relation's own structure, not by a hash, not by an authority.

The slot is computed in three layers:

  • fano7 — which Fano line (0-6), from structural incidence
  • role3 — which chirality role (0-2), from diagonal phase
  • local240 — fine position (0-239), from quadratic form

7 × 3 × 240 = 5040. No hash. No lookup. The structure IS the address.
